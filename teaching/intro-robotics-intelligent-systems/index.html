Introduction to Robotics and Intelligent Systems

Fourth-year BSc course in Software Engineering at Addis Ababa University, 2026.

My role: lecturer and lab assistant, teaching the lectures and running the weekly labs.

An introduction to how mobile robots move, sense and decide. Each topic is taught in a lecture and then built in a weekly lab on a simulated differential-drive robot in ROS 2 and Gazebo.

Topics

  • Robot motion and control
  • Sensing
  • Kalman filtering
  • Localization and mapping
  • Path planning

Labs

Lab Topic Materials
1 ROS 2 and custom robot simulation: ROS 2 concepts, a differential-drive robot in Gazebo, and nodes that use topics, services and actions lab_01

A new lab is added for every session. All lab material is in intro_robotics_intelligent_systems_labs.

Requirements

Ubuntu 22.04 with ROS 2 Humble, and Gazebo Harmonic with its ROS 2 packages.
