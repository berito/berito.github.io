Overview

I’m a robotics engineer in SLAM, navigation and computer vision, with more than four years of work on robot localization and mapping. I build robot software in ROS 2 with C++ and Python.

I’m completing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at Addis Ababa University, with a thesis on robust pose-graph optimization. I have taught Electrical and Computer Engineering at Samara University since 2021, and I teach robotics part-time at Addis Ababa University.

Focus areas

Computer Vision / Perception

Robot perception from camera and 3D sensor data: object detection and tracking, segmentation, multi-sensor fusion and real-time inference on the robot.

SLAM and State Estimation

Localization, mapping and state estimation: LiDAR and visual-inertial SLAM, pose-graph optimization, sensor fusion and calibration.

Robotics and Robot Learning

Autonomy for mobile robots: navigation, motion planning and control, and robot learning, including imitation and reinforcement learning.

Work with me

I take freelance projects in SLAM, navigation, perception and robot simulation, and work remotely with clients and teams anywhere. For a project, message me on Upwork; for roles and collaboration, on LinkedIn.

Videos

On my YouTube channel, LearnBotics, I show how my projects work, from the design to the test results. The latest series is the Drone Pacer: a PX4 drone that paces a runner around a track.
