**My role:** lab assistant, running the weekly labs.

The lab part of the MSc course: students build what the lectures cover on simulated robots in ROS&nbsp;2 and Gazebo, from a first robot model to
perception, SLAM and arm motion planning.

## Topics

- Robot simulation in ROS&nbsp;2 and Gazebo
- Perception
- Sensor fusion and SLAM
- Robot arm motion planning with MoveIt&nbsp;2

## Labs

| Lab | Topic                                                                                                                                              | Materials                                                                              |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | ROS&nbsp;2 and custom robot simulation: ROS&nbsp;2 concepts, a differential-drive robot in Gazebo, and nodes that use topics, services and actions | [lab_01](https://github.com/berito/robotics_intelligent_systems_labs/tree/main/lab_01) |
| 2   | Perception, sensor fusion and SLAM: object detection and segmentation with pretrained YOLO models on the robot's camera                            | [lab_02](https://github.com/berito/robotics_intelligent_systems_labs/tree/main/lab_02) |

A new lab is added for every session. All lab material is in
[robotics_intelligent_systems_labs](https://github.com/berito/robotics_intelligent_systems_labs).

## Requirements

Ubuntu 22.04 with ROS&nbsp;2 Humble, and Gazebo Harmonic with its ROS&nbsp;2 packages.
