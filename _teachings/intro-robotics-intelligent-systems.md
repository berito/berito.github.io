**My role:** lecturer and lab assistant, teaching the lectures and running the weekly labs.

An introduction to how mobile robots move, sense and decide. Each topic is taught in a lecture and then built in a weekly lab on a simulated
differential-drive robot in ROS&nbsp;2 and Gazebo.

## Topics

- Robot motion and control
- Sensing
- Kalman filtering
- Localization and mapping
- Path planning

## Labs

| Lab | Topic                                                                                                                                              | Materials                                                                                    |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | ROS&nbsp;2 and custom robot simulation: ROS&nbsp;2 concepts, a differential-drive robot in Gazebo, and nodes that use topics, services and actions | [lab_01](https://github.com/berito/intro_robotics_intelligent_systems_labs/tree/main/lab_01) |

A new lab is added for every session. All lab material is in
[intro_robotics_intelligent_systems_labs](https://github.com/berito/intro_robotics_intelligent_systems_labs).

## Requirements

Ubuntu 22.04 with ROS&nbsp;2 Humble, and Gazebo Harmonic with its ROS&nbsp;2 packages.
