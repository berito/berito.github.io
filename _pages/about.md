## Overview

I'm a robotics engineer in SLAM, navigation and computer vision, with more than four years of work on robot localization and mapping. I build robot
software in ROS&nbsp;2 with C++ and Python.

I'm completing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at Addis Ababa University, with a thesis on robust pose-graph optimization. I have taught Electrical
and Computer Engineering at Samara University since 2021, and I teach robotics part-time at Addis Ababa University.
